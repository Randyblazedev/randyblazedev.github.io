From Curiosity to Code

Every developer has a story behind how they started coding. Mine started with curiosity — and a burning need to understand what was happening behind the screen.

I was always interested in technology and wanted to understand how things worked behind the screen. I started wondering how websites, applications, and digital tools were created. That curiosity pushed me to explore programming and discover the world of web development.

This is the story of how a simple question — "how does this work?" — turned into a journey that's still going.

Chapter 1 — The First Step: HTML

My first step was learning HTML. It helped me understand the foundation of websites and how different elements come together to create a webpage. At first it seemed like a puzzle — tags, attributes, and nesting. But the moment I opened a file in a browser and saw my own heading appear on screen, something clicked. I had created something from nothing. 

<h1>Hello, I'm Randy</h1>
<p>This is my first webpage.</p>
"The moment I saw my own heading appear on screen, something clicked. I had created something from nothing."

Chapter 2 — Expanding Beyond the Basics

After learning the basics, I started exploring more parts of frontend development and discovered how creative building websites could be. I began learning how to design better interfaces, make pages responsive, and create experiences that users could enjoy. Tailwind CSS became my favorite tool — it let me build beautiful layouts without fighting with stylesheets.

Chapter 3 — The Struggles Were the Lessons

The journey was not always easy. There were times when my code had errors, my designs did not look right, or I struggled to understand certain concepts. But every problem became a learning opportunity. Debugging helped me understand code better and improve my problem-solving skills. I learned to read error messages instead of being scared of them — they're actually the browser telling you exactly where to look.

"I learned to read error messages instead of being scared of them. They're the browser telling you exactly where to look."

🏁 Milestone

Building my own projects was the turning point — it changed me from someone learning concepts into someone creating real experiences on the web.

Chapter 4 — From Concepts to Real Projects

As I continued learning, I started building my own projects and turning ideas into real websites. Those projects helped me grow into someone who could create real experiences on the web. Coding became more than just a skill — it became a way for me to create, experiment, solve problems, and share ideas.

Chapter 5 — The Journey Continues

My journey is still continuing. There are many technologies I want to learn — JavaScript, React, and beyond. More projects I want to build, and more challenges I want to overcome. Every developer starts somewhere. The important thing is to keep learning, keep creating, and keep improving.

🧠 The Lesson

You don't need to know everything before you start building. Every developer starts somewhere. The important thing is to keep learning, keep creating, and keep improving.
